01 — About Me
Who I Am

Designer.
Developer.
Creative.

I'm a final-year MSc Cybersecurity student, passionate about crafting digital experiences that are as functional as they are beautiful. My work spans UI/UX design, full-stack web development, and creative coding — where logic meets emotion.

Throughout my studies, I've developed a deep appreciation for human-centred design and clean engineering — from building complete e-commerce platforms to designing polished app prototypes and client-facing websites.

My design philosophy is rooted in the belief that the best digital products feel inevitable — so natural and intuitive that users forget someone built them. I build with empathy, iterate with purpose, and ship with craft.

I believe in "emotionally durable design" — creating interfaces that remain satisfying and usable long after the first interaction. This means prioritizing accessibility, performance, and timeless aesthetics over fleeting trends.
